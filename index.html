Huntsville.

Hello, I’m Ehi! I’ve been making things for as long as I can remember, from building elaborate LEGO pieces and designing flyers at 9, to designing architectural floor plans in PowerPoint at 12. At 15, I moved from The Gambia to The United States for college. Starting over in a new country taught me to become comfortable with unfamiliarity, pay attention to people and context, and approach problems with curiosity and empathy. Now, at 18, I study Computer Science at Alabama A&M University. My work is grounded by the belief that technology should be intentional, human-centered, and a little whimsical.

Outside of design, I love to travel to new places, try new recipes, read novels and watch documentaries.

P.S. I’m available for 2027 internship opportunities!
